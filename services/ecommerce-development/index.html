Ecommerce Development FAQs

How long does it take to build an online store?

It depends on the number of products, features, and integrations required. We set the timeline clearly in the proposal and split the work into stages you review as each one is delivered.

How is AI changing the way people buy from online stores?

Many customers now ask AI assistants and search engines about the best product before buying, and these platforms show the products whose data they understand clearly: name, price, availability, and reviews.

That's why Zero2One builds product pages with structured data and clear descriptions, so your store is ready to appear on Google, in Shopping ads, and on AI platforms, not just on your own site.

Does store design affect your products' visibility in search engines (SEO)?

Yes, significantly. Store speed, mobile compatibility, and the structure of product and category pages all determine whether your products appear on Google. A badly built store can hide your products no matter how good they are.

That's why Zero2One builds every store on an SEO-ready technical foundation from day one, making it a strong starting point for SEO and ad campaigns after launch.

Which package is right for me, and how much does it cost to build an online store?

The cost of building a store depends on the number of products, the features, and integrations with payment, shipping, and other systems. We start by understanding your needs, then recommend the right package and send a detailed proposal.

What's the best way to build an online store: a ready-made platform or a custom store?

Ready-made platforms suit a quick start and small stores, while a custom store suits businesses that need special features, full control, or significant growth. We help you choose based on your size and goals.

How do I choose the best ecommerce development company?

Choose a company that shows real stores, starts by understanding how you work, hands over the store and its accounts in your name, and provides support after launch. Ask about these four points before you sign.
