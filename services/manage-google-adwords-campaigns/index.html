Google Ads Management FAQs

Is a Commercial Registration Required to Advertise on Google Ads?

Yes. Google Ads requires a valid Commercial Registration (CR) for advertising business activities in Saudi Arabia. The Commercial Registration helps verify the business and ensures compliance with Google’s requirements and applicable local regulations.

How much budget do I need to start Google Ads?

There's no single number that suits everyone; the budget depends on your industry, location, and cost per click in your sector. After studying your business, we suggest a suitable starting budget, then gradually increase it on the campaigns that prove themselves.

How do you charge for campaign management?

Our management fee is separate from your ad budget, which is paid directly to Google. We show both separately in the proposal before we start.

When do Google Ads results show?

Campaigns start bringing visits and calls from the first days. The first weeks are for gathering data and testing what works, then the cost per customer improves with ongoing optimization.

How is AI used in Google Ads?

Google now relies on AI for bidding (Smart Bidding) and for Performance Max campaigns, which automatically choose where and when your ad appears across Search, YouTube, Maps, and the Display Network.

But these tools only work well with accurate tracking data and careful setup. That's why Zero2One combines the power of Google's AI with continuous human oversight, making sure the algorithm learns from real customers, not random clicks.

Do I need a website or landing page before advertising?

Yes. The ad brings the customer to your door, and the page is what convinces them to get in touch. A slow or unclear page wastes budget with every click and lowers Google's quality rating for your ad, raising your cost per click.

If you don't have a suitable website, Zero2One designs fast, conversion-focused landing pages connected to tracking tools, so your advertising campaign starts on the right foundation from day one.

How do I choose the best Google Ads management company?

Choose a company that creates the account in your name, starts by analyzing your business and competitors, measures success by customers rather than clicks, and sends clear reports. Ask about these four points before you sign.
