Marketing Consulting FAQs

How long does it take to prepare a marketing plan?

It depends on the size of the project and the number of channels to study. We set the timeline clearly in the proposal before we start, and keep you updated at every stage.

Does the plan cover search and AI?

Yes. Customers now search for products and services on Google and AI platforms before buying, so the plan defines how your brand appears there, alongside social media and ads.

Zero2One draws on its daily experience in SEO and AI visibility to build a plan that matches how your customers search today, not how they searched years ago.

Do I have to carry out the plan with you?

No. The plan is yours, and you can carry it out with your team or any agency you choose; it's written clearly enough for any team to execute.

If you'd like, Zero2One can carry it out with you fully or on specific channels, because our team offers every service the plan covers in one place.

Which package is right for me?

It depends on what you need: a consulting session for a specific question, a full marketing plan, or a plan with periodic follow-up. We start by understanding your situation, then recommend the best fit.

Do I need consulting, or should I start executing right away?

If you know your audience and channels and your results are clear, start executing. But if you're spending without knowing what works, or starting a new project, consulting saves you months and wasted budget.

How do I choose among the best marketing consulting firms?

Choose a firm that builds its plan on your market data rather than generic templates, has real execution experience, hands over a written plan with KPIs, and doesn't tie you to executing with them. Ask about these four points before you sign.
