Social Media Campaign Management FAQs

Which platform suits my business: Snapchat, TikTok, or Instagram?

+

It depends on your audience and product. Snapchat and TikTok are strong for reaching young audiences in Saudi Arabia, Instagram suits image-led brands, and LinkedIn suits businesses. We choose the right platforms with you after studying your audience.

How much budget do I need for social media ads?

+

The budget depends on the platforms, audience size, and goal. We suggest a suitable starting budget, and our management fee is separate from the budget paid directly to the platforms.

Do you design the ads and produce the videos?

+

Yes. Our team designs the ads, writes the copy, and produces short videos in your audience's dialect, and sends them for your approval before publishing.

How does AI help with social media ads?

+

Platforms now rely on AI for targeting and budget allocation, such as Advantage+ campaigns on Meta and Smart+ on TikTok, which automatically find the people most likely to buy.

But the algorithm only succeeds with strong content and accurate tracking data. That's why Zero2One combines each platform's AI tools with creative content and continuous human oversight, so your campaign learns from your real customers.

What's the difference between account management and ad campaign management?

+

Account management means regular posting, engaging with your current followers, and building your brand's presence. Ad campaign management means paid reach to new audiences beyond your followers, with a specific goal such as messages or sales.

It works best when both run together: an active account that builds trust, and ads that bring new customers to it. That's why Zero2One offers both services in one team, keeping content and advertising aligned.

What Are the Best Practices for Managing Social Media Advertising Campaigns?

+

The best practices for managing social media advertising campaigns include defining the target audience, choosing the right platforms, and creating engaging and relevant ads. A professional advertising agency can help businesses develop an effective advertising campaign based on their goals, audience, and budget.

Using creative advertising concepts, testing different ad formats, monitoring campaign performance, and continuously optimizing targeting and budgets can also help improve results and maximize return on ad spend.

What Are the Responsibilities of an Advertising Agency?

+

The responsibilities of an advertising agency depend on the services and scope of work agreed upon with the client. In general, an agency may be responsible for developing and managing an advertising campaign, defining the target audience, creating ad content, managing budgets, monitoring campaign performance, and making ongoing optimizations.

For businesses targeting other companies, an agency can also develop and manage B2B advertising campaigns designed to generate qualified leads and support business growth.

Can I Manage Facebook Advertising Campaigns Myself?

+

Yes, businesses can manage Facebook advertising campaigns themselves, but doing so requires a good understanding of Meta's advertising tools, audience targeting, campaign objectives, budgeting, and performance analysis.

Effective management also requires regular testing, optimization, and keeping up with changes to advertising platforms and best practices. Working with an experienced advertising agency can help businesses save time and improve campaign performance, especially when managing complex or large-scale advertising campaigns.
