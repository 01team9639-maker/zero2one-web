Frequently Asked Questions About Website Design & Development

How long does it take to design and build a website?

The timeline depends on the number of pages, features, and functionality required. A standard corporate website usually takes less time than a more complex website. We clearly define the estimated timeline in your proposal and divide the project into stages, allowing you to review each stage upon delivery.

Can I request changes to the design?

Yes. We review the design with you throughout the process and make the required adjustments to ensure the final design meets your expectations before development begins.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. We consider SEO fundamentals from the website development stage by creating a technically sound structure that follows search engine best practices and supports better visibility in Google search results.

What is an AI-ready website?

An AI-ready website is built in a way that helps AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Gemini read, understand, and reference its content in their answers. This depends on a sound technical structure, clear and well-organized content, and structured data (Schema) that helps these platforms understand who you are and what you offer.

This does not replace good design or traditional SEO. Instead, it builds on them. As customers increasingly use AI assistants for research and comparison, Zero2One builds websites that are prepared for visibility across both Google and AI platforms.

Does website design affect SEO and AI visibility?

Yes, significantly. Website speed, mobile responsiveness, page structure, and headings are all factors Google considers when ranking websites. A poorly built website can limit your visibility regardless of how good your content is.

A clear structure and well-organized content also help AI platforms understand and potentially recommend your website. That's why Zero2One builds every website on a technically SEO-ready foundation from day one, creating a strong starting point for future marketing campaigns.

Which package is right for me?

This depends on the number of pages and features your business requires. We start by understanding your goals and then recommend the package that best fits your project and budget.

Should I build a new website or improve my existing one?

If your current website has a sound technical foundation, it may be possible to improve its design and performance. However, if it is slow, outdated, or difficult to manage, rebuilding it is often more cost-effective in the long term. We review your website and recommend the most suitable option.
