Mobile App Development FAQs

How long does it take to develop a mobile app?

It depends on the number of screens, features, and integrations required. We set the timeline clearly in the proposal and split the work into stages you review as each one is delivered.

Can AI be added to the app?

Yes. Smart features can be added, such as an assistant that answers customer questions, product suggestions based on each user's interests, and smart in-app search.

During analysis, we define with you the features that add real value for your users, because Zero2One doesn't add AI as decoration, but where it makes the customer experience easier and improves results.

Do I need an app, or is a website enough?

A website is essential for every business to appear on Google and receive new customers. An app suits businesses that customers deal with repeatedly, such as ordering, booking, or follow-ups.

That's why Zero2One starts by understanding how your customers interact with you, then advises you on the best fit: a website, an app, or both.

Which package is right for me?

It depends on the number of screens and features, and whether you need a control panel and integrations with other systems. We start by understanding your idea, then recommend the right package.

Should I start with a full version or a simple first version?

We usually recommend starting with a first version that has the core features, then developing it based on real customer usage. This reduces cost and risk and speeds up launch.

How do I choose the best app development company?

Choose a company that analyzes your idea before coding, splits the project into clear stages, hands over the app, its accounts, and its source code in your name, and provides support after launch. Ask about these four points before you sign.
