Web Design & Development FAQs

How long does it take to design and build a website?

+

It depends on the number of pages and the features you need. A company profile website usually takes less time than a full-featured one. We set the timeline clearly in the proposal and split the work into stages you review as each one is delivered.

What is an AI-ready website?

+

It's a website built so AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Gemini can read its content, understand it, and reference it in their answers. This relies on a sound technical structure, clear and organized content, and structured data (Schema) that tells these platforms who you are and what you offer.

It doesn't replace good design or traditional SEO; it builds on them. As customers rely more and more on AI assistants to search and compare, Zero2One builds its clients' websites to be ready to appear on both Google and AI platforms.

Does website design affect visibility in search engines (SEO) and AI?

+

Yes, significantly. Site speed, mobile compatibility, page structure, and headings are all factors Google uses to rank websites. A badly built website can block your visibility no matter how good your content is.

A clear structure and organized content also help AI platforms understand and recommend your website. That's why Zero2One builds every website on an SEO-ready technical foundation from day one, making it a strong starting point for any marketing campaign that follows.

Which package is right for me?

+

It depends on the number of pages and features your business needs. We start by understanding your goals, then recommend the package that fits your project and budget.

Should I build a new website or improve my current one?

+

If your current website is technically sound, it can be improved and redesigned. If it's slow, outdated, or hard to update, rebuilding it is usually more cost-effective in the long run. We review your website and tell you which option suits you best.

How do I choose the best website development company in Riyadh?

+

Choose a company that shows real work, starts by understanding your business before designing, hands over the website and its accounts in your name, and provides support after launch. Ask about these four points before you sign.

Brand Identity Design FAQs

How long does it take to design a complete brand identity?

+

It depends on the scope of work and the number of applications needed. We set the timeline and stages clearly in the proposal before we start, and you review each stage as it's delivered.

How do you design a complete brand identity?

+

A complete identity starts with strategy, not the logo: defining the audience, positioning against competitors, and the brand's personality and message. Then comes design: the logo, colors, typography, and visual elements, followed by tone of voice.

The identity is completed by applying it to real uses, such as print, social media, and the website, and documenting it in brand guidelines. This is how Zero2One works with every client, so the identity is a complete system, not a standalone logo.

Does brand identity affect your brand's visibility on Google and AI?

+

Yes, indirectly. When your brand's name, logo, and description appear consistently across your website, accounts, and Google Business Profile, it becomes easier for search engines and AI platforms to recognize it as one trusted brand.

A clear identity also increases customer trust and clicks on your name in results. That's why Zero2One builds the identity alongside the website and SEO, so your brand shows up just as strongly everywhere.

Which package is right for me?

+

It depends on your project's stage and the number of applications you need. We start by understanding your business, then recommend the package that fits your goals and budget.

Should I design a new identity or develop my current one?

+

If your current identity has elements your customers already recognize, it's usually best to develop it and keep what deserves to stay. If it doesn't reflect your business at all, starting fresh is the better option. We review your identity and tell you honestly.

How do I choose the best branding and identity design company?

+

Choose a company that starts with strategy before design, shows real work, applies the identity to your business's actual uses, and hands over the brand guidelines and all source files. Ask about these four points before you sign.

Social Media Campaign Management FAQs

Which platform suits my business: Snapchat, TikTok, or Instagram?

+

It depends on your audience and product. Snapchat and TikTok are strong for reaching young audiences in Saudi Arabia, Instagram suits image-led brands, and LinkedIn suits businesses. We choose the right platforms with you after studying your audience.

How much budget do I need for social media ads?

+

The budget depends on the platforms, audience size, and goal. We suggest a suitable starting budget, and our management fee is separate from the budget paid directly to the platforms.

Do you design the ads and produce the videos?

+

Yes. Our team designs the ads, writes the copy, and produces short videos in your audience's dialect, and sends them for your approval before publishing.

How does AI help with social media ads?

+

Platforms now rely on AI for targeting and budget allocation, such as Advantage+ campaigns on Meta and Smart+ on TikTok, which automatically find the people most likely to buy.

But the algorithm only succeeds with strong content and accurate tracking data. That's why Zero2One combines each platform's AI tools with creative content and continuous human oversight, so your campaign learns from your real customers.

What's the difference between account management and ad campaign management?

+

Account management means regular posting, engaging with your current followers, and building your brand's presence. Ad campaign management means paid reach to new audiences beyond your followers, with a specific goal such as messages or sales.

It works best when both run together: an active account that builds trust, and ads that bring new customers to it. That's why Zero2One offers both services in one team, keeping content and advertising aligned.

What Are the Best Practices for Managing Social Media Advertising Campaigns?

+

The best practices for managing social media advertising campaigns include defining the target audience, choosing the right platforms, and creating engaging and relevant ads. A professional advertising agency can help businesses develop an effective advertising campaign based on their goals, audience, and budget.

Using creative advertising concepts, testing different ad formats, monitoring campaign performance, and continuously optimizing targeting and budgets can also help improve results and maximize return on ad spend.

What Are the Responsibilities of an Advertising Agency?

+

The responsibilities of an advertising agency depend on the services and scope of work agreed upon with the client. In general, an agency may be responsible for developing and managing an advertising campaign, defining the target audience, creating ad content, managing budgets, monitoring campaign performance, and making ongoing optimizations.

For businesses targeting other companies, an agency can also develop and manage B2B advertising campaigns designed to generate qualified leads and support business growth.

Can I Manage Facebook Advertising Campaigns Myself?

+

Yes, businesses can manage Facebook advertising campaigns themselves, but doing so requires a good understanding of Meta's advertising tools, audience targeting, campaign objectives, budgeting, and performance analysis.

Effective management also requires regular testing, optimization, and keeping up with changes to advertising platforms and best practices. Working with an experienced advertising agency can help businesses save time and improve campaign performance, especially when managing complex or large-scale advertising campaigns.

Google Ads Management FAQs

Is a Commercial Registration Required to Advertise on Google Ads?

+

Yes. Google Ads requires a valid Commercial Registration (CR) for advertising business activities in Saudi Arabia. The Commercial Registration helps verify the business and ensures compliance with Google’s requirements and applicable local regulations.

How much budget do I need to start Google Ads?

+

There's no single number that suits everyone; the budget depends on your industry, location, and cost per click in your sector. After studying your business, we suggest a suitable starting budget, then gradually increase it on the campaigns that prove themselves.

How do you charge for campaign management?

+

Our management fee is separate from your ad budget, which is paid directly to Google. We show both separately in the proposal before we start.

When do Google Ads results show?

+

Campaigns start bringing visits and calls from the first days. The first weeks are for gathering data and testing what works, then the cost per customer improves with ongoing optimization.

How is AI used in Google Ads?

+

Google now relies on AI for bidding (Smart Bidding) and for Performance Max campaigns, which automatically choose where and when your ad appears across Search, YouTube, Maps, and the Display Network.

But these tools only work well with accurate tracking data and careful setup. That's why Zero2One combines the power of Google's AI with continuous human oversight, making sure the algorithm learns from real customers, not random clicks.

Do I need a website or landing page before advertising?

+

Yes. The ad brings the customer to your door, and the page is what convinces them to get in touch. A slow or unclear page wastes budget with every click and lowers Google's quality rating for your ad, raising your cost per click.

If you don't have a suitable website, Zero2One designs fast, conversion-focused landing pages connected to tracking tools, so your advertising campaign starts on the right foundation from day one.

How do I choose the best Google Ads management company?

+

Choose a company that creates the account in your name, starts by analyzing your business and competitors, measures success by customers rather than clicks, and sends clear reports. Ask about these four points before you sign.

SEO FAQs

When do SEO results start to show?

+

SEO results usually begin to appear within three to six months, depending on your website's condition and the strength of the competition. Every month, we share reports and early indicators with you, such as indexing, website traffic, and keyword rankings.

What is AI SEO?

+

AI SEO means optimizing your website so it appears in the answers of AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Gemini. It focuses on strengthening these platforms' trust in your brand, the quality of your content, and your website's technical structure. It complements traditional SEO and makes sure you reach customers who rely on AI for their buying decisions.

AI SEO doesn't replace traditional SEO; it builds on its strong foundations. As consumers rely more and more on AI assistants for buying decisions, Zero2One combines traditional search engine optimization on Google with AI optimization so its clients appear strongly across every platform, backed by advanced tools and a specialist team that tests the latest strategies.

Do articles help improve your website's visibility in search engines (SEO) and AI?

+

Yes. Blog posts support SEO when they're built on a clear strategy. Publishing useful, targeted content that addresses your audience's needs strengthens your site's topical authority and attracts visitors interested in your services.

Well-structured content also helps AI platforms understand your information and reference it easily. To get the most from it, Zero2One uses article writing as part of a comprehensive strategy that includes technical optimization, internal linking, and building your site's credibility.

Which package is right for me?

+

It depends on the size of your website and how competitive your industry is. We start by analyzing your website, then recommend the package that fits your goals.

Should I start with SEO or ads?

+

Ads bring quick traffic but stop when you stop paying, while SEO builds visibility that grows over time. Many businesses combine both.
